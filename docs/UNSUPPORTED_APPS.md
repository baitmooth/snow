# Unsupported Apps

This document lists applications for which icons will not be designed or included in this pack. I reserve the right to exclude certain apps without further comment or explanation.

## Reasons for Exclusion

Exclusions are typically based on ethical, social, or political considerations, including but not limited to:

*   **Financing Aggression:** Apps that are directly or indirectly linked to the financing of the war of aggression against Ukraine (e.g., Wildberries).
*   **Promotion of Hate and Fascism:** Apps that promote, facilitate, or provide a platform for fascist, extremist, or similar ideologies that contradict our values of inclusivity and peace (e.g., Truth Social).

## Excluded Applications

The following apps are currently excluded from this icon pack:

*   **Wildberries** (Reason: Financing of war)
*   **Truth Social** (Reason: Promotion of extremist ideologies)
